# Accessing the studios

The studios can be used by students of the Audio Communication and Technology programme to work on projects related to their studies, regularly between 10:00-22:00 on any weekday.

!!! warning "Important"
    a studio introduction is mandatory for working in the studios. these happen once per semester and will be announced in the ak-students mailing list. 
    
    **You also need to have booked the studios before using them!**

In order to book the studios send a mail containing your **name**, the **project** you are working on and your desired **timeslots** to [studio@ak.tu-berlin.de](mailto:studio@ak.tu-berlin.de), we will then reply to your request.
!!! info "Checking available timeslots"
    Available timeslots can be found in the [calendar](https://cloudy.ak.tu-berlin.de/index.php/apps/calendar/p/iyJAXGtyaFt5sANH-Ea3mqg5mog2jeST9).

## Getting the key

When you have booked a timeslot you can get the key to the studios at the gatekeepers office, using your (student-)id to prove your identity.
The gatekeeper is found next to the [main entrance to the E-N building](https://www.openstreetmap.org/?mlat=52.514761&mlon=13.326443#map=19/52.514761/13.326443&layers=N):

<iframe width="100%" height="350" src="https://www.openstreetmap.org/export/embed.html?bbox=13.325431644916536%2C52.51404668925007%2C13.327454030513765%2C52.5154749317376&amp;layer=mapnik&amp;marker=52.51476081629669%2C13.326442837715149" style="border: 1px solid black"></iframe>

!!! info "Updating the key"
    After getting the key you should always update it in the update box in the gatekeepers office, right below the window to the office

!!! warning "Important"
    Always return the key after finishing in the studios, under no circumstance is it allowed to take it home!
